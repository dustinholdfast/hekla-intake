Marketplace pull list

Cards that sold on CardTrader or starfoil.store, to take down on TCGplayer by hand. The import CSV uses TCGplayer's pricing-export format (Seller Portal → Pricing → Import, then review in Staged). It subtracts the sold copies with Add to Quantity, and it also sets each card's price to Hekla's market price, because TCGplayer requires a price column. Only cards with a TCGplayer SKU in Airtable can go in the file; the rest are in the checklist.
