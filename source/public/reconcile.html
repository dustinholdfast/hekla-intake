CardTrader cleanup → Airtable

After merging or deleting listings on CardTrader: the row that owns each surviving listing takes CardTrader's quantity, and duplicate rows whose listings are gone are removed from Airtable. The TCGplayer import uses Add to Quantity = Airtable − TCGplayer and keeps your current TCGplayer price (TCGplayer requires a price column). Only SKU or confirmed matches go in it. Upload it at Seller Portal → Pricing → Import, then review in Staged.

A row leaves this list as soon as it is reconciled. Rows in a downloaded TCGplayer import are marked pending upload and hidden until a newer TCGplayer export is uploaded (which then shows whether they match) or 24 hours pass. Tick Show reconciled to see them all.

Reconcile All treats Airtable as the truth for every mismatch in this view (game and platform filters apply): it sets CardTrader to the Airtable quantity and builds one TCGplayer import CSV. It never changes Airtable. You see exactly what will happen before anything changes.
